# TKN 32 km — fuel plan (Sat 17 Oct, start 11:00)

Pace 7:45–8:00 min/km → finish ~4 h 05–4 h 15.
Aid: **km 7 water**, **km 13.2 food + water**, **km 24 food + water**. No cups — bring foldable cup.

- **9 × Nutrend CarboSnack** (31 g carbs, 150 mg sodium each): 4 lemon, 3 blueberry, 2 cola (caffeine 60 mg)
- **5 × Extrifit Salt Caps** (250 mg sodium each; max 5/day)
- Bladder **1 l** water (fill to full at every refill), foldable cup, windproof jacket, phone, ID, watch with GPX

## Rules
- Water: 2–3 sips every km (~0.5–0.6 l/h). Km 13.2→24 is the longest gap: drink steadily but not more — 1 l covers it. If it runs dry early, a cup of cola at km 24 first. Wash each gel down with 3–4 sips.
- Go by **km marks**, not hunger. Don't skip km 4 / 7 / 10 — early fuel saves km 20–30.
- Dunes (km 20–27) are slow — gels come further apart in time; that's fine.
- Cramping → 1 salt cap immediately (stay ≤ 5 total).
- Stomach heavy → swap one gel for cola sips or ½ banana at the next station.
- Nothing new on race day.

## After
- Within 1 h: **30–40 g protein + carbs** (curd/yoghurt + fruit, or a proper meal).
- Dinner: normal big plate (meat/fish + rice/potatoes + veg). 1–2 planned beers at the afterparty are fine, then water.
- Sunday: maintenance ~2300–2500 kcal, ~150 g protein. Monday: back to the usual deficit. Scale +1–2 kg until ~Tuesday = glycogen + water.
